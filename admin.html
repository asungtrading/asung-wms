Putaway — lines received in this period

⚠ Periods before 2026-08-04 cannot be trusted for "Putaway done". Until then the only way to mark a line placed was one tap per line, and on receipts with more than about 10 lines nobody used it — those lines read as backlog even though the stock was put away. Bin-level "Place all" was added on 2026-08-04; compare rates only from that date on.

⚠ "Moved in Cin7" counts transfer lines with exported_base > 0. exported_base is only an approximation of what the WMS actually moved — it can be polluted by manual updates (2026-07-28: 344 rows of TR-02935 were backfilled by hand). Since 2026-07-31 PO lines also record exported_base (there it means "put on the Cin7 stock received document"), but they are not counted here — this metric stays transfer-only.
